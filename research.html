Method priorities

Evidence before narrative

Reproducibility and robustness are treated as part of the scientific result, not merely as implementation details.

  • Explicit analysis boundaries
  • Versioned code and configurations
  • Synthetic and observational checks
  • Held-out validation where applicable
  • Claims proportional to evidence
